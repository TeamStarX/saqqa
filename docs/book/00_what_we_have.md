### The one-paragraph version

Saqqa is a working prototype that verifies a trucked-water delivery through the operator's mobile network and a sensor on the buyer's tank, decides what should be paid for, and writes down why. It runs live against Nokia Network as Code: in the most recent full run, twelve seeded trips made **{{stats.calls_total}}** real CAMARA calls with **{{stats.errors}}** errors (median **{{stats.latency_median}} ms**), every geofence subscription produced a real CloudEvent at our webhook, and every trip decided the way the scenario expected. The model in the loop is Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite; it plans, investigates and explains. It never decides money.

### What is real, simulated, or stubbed

<div class="callout">
<p><strong>Real:</strong> every CAMARA call, its request and its response; the CloudEvents the operator platform posts to our webhook; the model's planning, investigation and audit notes; the deterministic decisions.</p>
<p><strong>Scripted:</strong> the trip timeline (when the truck entered and left each zone). Sandbox simulators have fixed positions and fire the subscribed event type on creation, so they cannot drive a truck through two zones. The timeline is the scenario's; the calls that attest to it are real, and the dashboard says which is which on every run.</p>
<p><strong>Simulated:</strong> the tank level and turbidity trace, from a pump model whose parameters are on screen. Hardware is Phase 3.</p>
<p><strong>Stubbed:</strong> the payment webhook to the payer's ERP (logged, not sent) and supervisor alerts.</p>
</div>
