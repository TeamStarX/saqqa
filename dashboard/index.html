Saqqaالسقّا network-attested delivery and payment for trucked water

Built on CAMARA Open Gateway APIs

A water delivery is paid for on the word of the person being paid.

Saqqa puts a sensor on the buyer's tank and a SIM in the tanker's cab, makes the mobile operator the witness, and pays only for the litres it can account for.

  • 12 of 12 seeded trips decide correctly
  • 125 live CAMARA calls, 0 errors
  • 3 APIs are enough to reach every verdict

What it takes to deploy

On the truck
Nothing. The SIM already in the cab is the tracker.
On the tank
One fixed level sensor with its own SIM, bought once by the buyer.
In software
One agent and three CAMARA APIs, running on Nokia Network as Code today.
In paperwork
The F-306 form stays; Saqqa fills it from the evidence.
In money
13.6¢ of network calls against a $30 trip.
